## Content Review

- [ ] Confirm every lesson is independent original material.
- [ ] Confirm no lesson imitates a known textbook, course, author, publisher, or brand.
- [ ] Confirm English lines are natural for A1 learners.
- [ ] Confirm Chinese translations are accurate and sentence-aligned.
- [ ] Confirm vocabulary choices match the lesson level.
- [ ] Confirm each multiple-choice question has one clear answer.
- [ ] Confirm dictation lines are useful and not too long.
- [ ] Confirm health-related language is framed as conversation practice, not medical advice.
- [ ] Confirm fictional personal names, phone numbers, and locations are generic and not tied to real private individuals.
- [ ] Confirm travel and airport lines are generic and do not imply a real airline or airport partner.
